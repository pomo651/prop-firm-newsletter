Platform pricing check - Nov 3, 2026 (America/Edmonton)
Source: beehiiv.com/pricing, support.substack.com, kit.com/pricing (and Kit secondary summary)

Beehiiv (Oct 2026 plans):
- Free: $0, up to 2,500 subscribers, unlimited sends, recommendation network
- Lite: from $49/mo yearly ($59 monthly) at 1,000 subs; up to 100,000; 0% take rate on paid subs; ads, automations, polls, A/B
- Pro: from $95/mo yearly ($109 monthly) at 1,000; up to 250,000; remove branding, more seats/pubs
- At Alpha 1 target (500 subs): Free fits. At 2,500 Free ceiling: still Free. Lite needed for paid subs / ad network / referral milestones.

Substack:
- Free to publish at any list size
- 10% of paid subscription revenue + Stripe (~2.9%+$0.30) + 0.7% Stripe Billing
- Custom domain: $50 one-time
- Strength: discovery network. Watch-out: take rate once paid.

Kit (ConvertKit):
- Free up to 10,000 subscribers
- Creator from ~$39/mo at 1,000 (scales with list); ~16% off yearly
- Commerce fee 3.5%+$0.30 (includes processing)
- Strength: automations/tagging. Watch-out: less built-in newsletter discovery than Beehiiv/Substack.

Recommendation unchanged: start Beehiiv Free for Alpha. Revisit Lite when monetizing or past ~2,500.
