01

Firms cut ties with Prop Firm Match over affiliate-tracking claims

On Oct 29, trader @traderkane posted a video on X showing that clicking a firm profile or a “copy code” button on Prop Firm Match (PFM) sent visitors through PFM’s affiliate link. On Oct 30, Apex, Tradeify, Topstep and Goat Funded Trader said they were ending their affiliate ties with PFM.

That evening PFM admitted the problem: “This should not have happened. We take full responsibility for it.” It says it is working through the numbers with each partner.

Disclosure: @traderkane’s X bio says he is building @propfirm.

Why it matters → Discount codes and “best firm” rankings often come with affiliate money attached. Pick firms on their rules, not a comparison site’s rank, and expect firms to tighten affiliate programs.

02

FundedNext sells a $50K CFD account with no challenge for $149.99

A limited Lab release (CFDs-FNL:004): 2,000 accounts in total, 5 per person. No daily loss limit, but the $2,000 max loss trails your highest balance in real time, and you must clear a fixed $2,100 buffer ($52,100) before withdrawing.

Each payout needs at least $800 profit above the buffer and is capped at $1,200; you keep 80%, so about $640 to $960 per payout. A 20% perpetual consistency rule applies, news-window profits count at 40%, and the account closes after the 5th payout. No refunds or resets.

Why it matters → The entry price is low, but the ceiling is about $4,800 per account (5 payouts of up to $960) and the trailing drawdown is tight. Run the numbers before you buy.

03

BrightFunded launches futures: BrightPlus and BrightCore

On Oct 26, CFD firm BrightFunded opened futures evaluations. Both plans are one phase: $25K to $150K accounts, a 6% target, no daily loss limit, no time limit, a one-time fee and no activation fee.

BrightPlus has an intraday drawdown and daily payouts; BrightCore has an end-of-day drawdown and pays every 5 trading days. Launch code BRIGHTFUTURES (50% off) puts the 25K at $60 (Plus) and $65 (Core).

Why it matters → Another CFD brand moves into futures after FTMO did in August. More competition usually means cheaper evaluations, but check the drawdown type: intraday is much easier to breach than end-of-day.

04

Rule changes at Alpha Futures, Funded Futures Network and Blue Guardian

•Alpha Futures: new optional “No Consistency in Qualified” add-on removes the 40% consistency rule on Standard funded accounts for +$70 / +$90 / +$110 a month (50K / 100K / 150K). The evaluation keeps its 50% rule.
•Funded Futures Network: the CEO says accounts bought after Oct 22 no longer get closed for HFT (short-trade) violations; only that day’s short-trade profit is removed when those trades exceed 30% of the day’s activity, per Funded Truth. Older accounts stay on the old terms.
•Blue Guardian Futures: the official rules page now lists a $5,000 end-of-day trailing max drawdown on the $150K Standard account, up from $3,500 per Funded Truth.

Why it matters → Almost every change applies only to accounts bought after the change date. Check your purchase date and the terms that came with it before you trade or request a payout.

05

Topstep: heavy account-buyers will be moved to Live

On Oct 28, Topstep replied on X to a trader upset about being moved to Live just before a payout. It said he had taken four payouts in three weeks while buying accounts “at a pace that annualizes to roughly 700 per year.”

“It isn’t a SIM lottery. Everyone is welcome, but repeatedly cycling through accounts will eventually mean proving your strategy in Live.” His XFA balances will be released into Live as he builds there. TopstepX also added Live performance stats.

Why it matters → Buying and cycling lots of accounts can trigger a Live call-up, which changes when and how you get paid. Read Topstep’s Live transition rules before you scale up.
