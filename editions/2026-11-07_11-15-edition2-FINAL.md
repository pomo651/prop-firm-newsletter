# Funded Tape: Top 5 Things You Can't Miss This Week (Nov 7 – Nov 15, 2026)

*A scalping rule got rewritten, a prop plan got a soccer-club partner, and the biggest ranking site says its list is now a sales chart.*

**Happy Monday, traders 👋**

This week Goat Funded Futures rewrote its holding-time rule, TopstepX had a Thursday-night data glitch (the platform is back up, but there is no public account resolution yet), Tradeify launched a plan with Inter Miami CF, Prop Firm Match changed how it ranks firms, and FundedNext dropped a same-day challenge from $20. Here are the 5 things you can't miss.

### 1. 📝 RULE BOOK

**Goat Funded Futures swaps its 2-minute rule for a 70% "1 minute rule" on every plan**

Since Mon Nov 9, every Goat Funded Futures (GFF) plan, from EOD and Flex to Daily Payouts, the Instant plans and GOAT Access, runs one holding-time check when you pass an evaluation and at every payout request: at least 70% of your closed trades, and at least 70% of your net profit, must come from trades held longer than 1 minute. Miss either and the payout is declined and that cycle's under-1-minute profit is deducted, though GFF says the account stays open.

It replaces the old per-trade 2-minute microscalping deduction, raises Flex's threshold from 50% to 70%, and applies to new and existing accounts; cycles through Sun Nov 8 stay on the old rules. Partial closes count as separate trades, and tick scalping is still banned on every account.

> 💡 **Why it matters:** *Scalpers: track your hold times per payout cycle. A cluster of sub-minute trades can now block a whole payout, even on an account you bought before Nov 9.*

Sources: [GFF holding-time rules](https://help.goatfundedfutures.com/en/articles/14130422-holding-time-rules-on-each-plan) · [GFF Flex rules](https://help.goatfundedfutures.com/en/articles/14888545-flex-challenge-specifications-rules-and-payouts) · [GFF on X](https://x.com/GFFutures/status/2121149304202985925)

### 2. 🎯 HEADS UP

**TopstepX had a data glitch Thursday night. If you were hit, don't touch the account yet**

Topstep says TopstepX trading data had an issue on Thu Nov 12 from 7:26 to 9:59 PM CT (8:26 to 10:59 PM ET) and later said the platform issue was fixed and TopstepX was back up. If you think your account was affected, Topstep asked you not to place trades, reset or delete the account until it follows up, because that could stop it from offering a resolution, and said you don't need to open a ticket.

On Fri Nov 13 Topstep told a trader on X it would email affected traders "later today with a resolution," and later Friday replies asked individual traders to DM their email. As of Sunday's 5 PM ET cutoff, Topstep hasn't publicly posted a resolution or said what went wrong or how many accounts were affected.

> 💡 **Why it matters:** *If you traded TopstepX Thursday evening and still haven't heard back, don't trade, reset or delete that account yet, and check any fix Topstep sends against your own trade log.*

Sources: [Topstep on X (status)](https://x.com/Topstep/status/2121094791823855705) · [Topstep on X (first alert)](https://x.com/Topstep/status/2121075303682638015) · [Topstep reply on X (Fri)](https://x.com/Topstep/status/2121283251142590670) · [Topstep later reply on X](https://x.com/Topstep/status/2121369309595213857)

### 3. 🚀 NEW LAUNCH

**Tradeify's Inter Miami "Miami Plan": one-day pass, static drawdown, and a hard $12,000 ending**

Tradeify became Inter Miami CF's Official Trading Partner on Nov 12 and launched the Miami Plan, a limited micros-only drop (10K, 25K, 50K) with no minimum days, no consistency rule, and a static drawdown that doesn't move even after payouts. A day later V2 joined V1: each payout splits 80% to you, 10% to a "live fund" and 10% to Tradeify (V1 is 75/10/15), with higher micro limits and lower targets on the 10K and 25K. Per the FAQ, V1 starts at $99 for the 10K with the optional soft daily loss limit.

The catch is in Tradeify's FAQ: Miami payout requests cap at $12,000 in total, and any profit above that is forfeited. Hit the cap and your funded Miami accounts close and you move to a separate $1,500 Miami live account (90/10, daily payouts), while every other Tradeify account you hold is paused until that live account breaches. KYC is required at purchase, and it's one buyer per household.

> 💡 **Why it matters:** *It's cheap and quick to pass, but it's designed to end at $12,000 in requests, about $9,600 in cash on V2 per Tradeify. Skip it if you need your other Tradeify accounts to keep running.*

Sources: [Tradeify Miami Plan FAQ](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/18370624-miami-plan-faq) · [Tradeify launch on X](https://x.com/Tradeify/status/2120933200679555146) · [Miami Plan 2.0 on X](https://x.com/Tradeify/status/2121276469582115029) · [Inter Miami partnership on X](https://x.com/Tradeify/status/2120885894538289588)

### 4. 🎯 HEADS UP

**Prop Firm Match's homepage now ranks its best-selling partners**

In a Nov 9 update to its statement, Prop Firm Match (PFM) said that with several firms pausing or ending partnerships, it no longer gets the same sales data and can't apply its old ranking (sales, views, ratings and favourites) consistently. Its homepage will now "prioritize our best-selling active partners," with a label, and PFM says a firm's position reflects its sales through PFM and partnership status and "should not be interpreted as an independent assessment" of the firm.

PFM says it will still show factual information on non-partner firms, and its review of the referral-redirect issue we covered last week is ongoing.

> 💡 **Why it matters:** *By PFM's own description, its homepage order is now a sales chart, not a quality ranking. Compare rules and payout terms on the firms' own pages.*

Sources: [PFM statement (Nov 9 update)](https://propfirmmatch.com/perpetuals/statement)

### 5. 🚀 NEW LAUNCH

**FundedNext Labs FNL:005: pick a $20 to $200 stake, hit one target the same day**

FundedNext's new CFD Labs drop, FNL:005, has you pick a $20, $50, $100 or $200 stake and a 3x or 5x multiplier, then trade a $10,000 account. Hit the target ($200 at 3x, $245 at 5x) before server close on the day you place your first trade, without losing $90 (3x) or $70 (5x), and you can request the reward that day: a multiple of your stake, up to $1,000 after KYC, per FundedNext.

There's no daily loss limit, but the Labs page lists a 50% best-trade rule, and it's capped at 5 accounts per user while slots last.

> 💡 **Why it matters:** *It's a one-day shot where the reward is a multiple of what you pay and the loss limit is tight. Read the best-trade rule before you try it.*

Sources: [FundedNext Labs page](https://fundednext.com/labs) · [FundedNext on X](https://x.com/FundedNext/status/2120857111215387075)
